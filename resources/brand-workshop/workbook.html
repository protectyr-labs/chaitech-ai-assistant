Brand DNA

Tools & Frameworks

Adapted with credit from the public Brand DNA workshop by Barry Hillier and Brian Hickling.

1 · Stakeholders

Everyone the brand serves, then the three most vital.
TASK A · Write all the different stakeholders you can think of for your business or brand. These typically include users, buyers, employees, board members, suppliers, family, general public, government, etc. Cast wide.
TASK B · From all your stakeholders, select the three most important or vital to your success.

3 · Purpose Archetype

Five ways a company makes the world better. Tick the one that fits best (and a secondary if useful).
Reduce Friction
Google · Spotify
Foster Prosperity
Pampers · Warby Parker
Enable Potential
Tesla · Nike
Encourage Exploration
Airbnb · Adobe
Kindle Happiness
Dove · Zappos
WHY · Why this archetype, not another? Write a real moment, a real customer, a real change you made for someone.

4 · Purpose Sentence · Long Form

Fill each field. The whole thing should read as a coherent paragraph when assembled.
We exist to
by
For the good of
How this impacts the people we serve
How we make money sustainably

5 · Purpose Words

20 to 35 words that describe how your brand is and acts. Circle the 5–8 most non-negotiable.

6 · Purpose Sentence · Short Form

A 3–7-word version of your purpose. North star, not tagline. Write three candidates; circle the strongest.

7 · Our Superpower

The intersection of what the world needs, what you are gifted at, and what you love.
What the world needs most
What we are uniquely gifted at
What we are passionate about
SUPERPOWER · Write the intersection in one strong sentence.

8 · Strategic Cascade

Roger Martin & A.G. Lafley · Playing to Win. Five interlocking choices.

9 · Strategic Pillars

Three or four things you must keep doing to live the purpose. Each pillar gets a short name AND a one-sentence "what this looks like in practice."
