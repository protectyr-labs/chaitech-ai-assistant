# Facilitator Guide · Brand Workshop

> For cohort leads, accelerator program managers, mentors, and founders running the workshop with a group of 4–20 participants.

## Format options

| Format | Time | Group size | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Half-day intensive** | 4 hours | 4–12 | Cohort kickoff, retreat day. Cover all 9 exercises in one sitting. |
| **Two evenings** | 2 × 2 hours | 4–20 | Working professionals. Evening 1 = Exercises 1–4. Evening 2 = Exercises 5–9. |
| **Eight weeks, one exercise per week** | 8 × 30 min | 6–20 | Ongoing programs. Slow build. Each exercise gets a critique session before the next. |
| **Solo + check-ins** | Async + 3 calls | 1 founder | High-touch coaching. Founder runs the skill; you review at exercises 4, 7, 9. |

## What you need

- A way for each founder to capture their workbook. Three options:
  - **Best:** Each founder runs the [Claude Code skill](../../skills/brand-workshop/) on their own machine. Live session is for discussion + critique only.
  - **Hybrid:** Print `workbook.html` for the session. Founder transfers answers to the skill afterward to get the exported deck.
  - **Paper only:** Print `workbook.html`. Skip the digital export.
- A whiteboard or large paper (for plenary discussion of one or two examples).
- Optional: have one filled example open for reference (`workbook-filled-example.html` works, or a real anonymized one from a past cohort).

## Session shape — half-day intensive

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 – 00:15 | Welcome | Frame the day. Show the filled example for 60 seconds. Set the rule: "you can change anything tomorrow; the goal today is a v1, not a final answer." |
| 00:15 – 00:45 | Exercise 1 — Stakeholders | 10 min individual, 10 min pair share, 10 min plenary critique on 1–2 volunteers. |
| 00:45 – 01:15 | Exercise 2 — Needs (FES) | Same shape as 1. Insist Functional / Emotional / Social are filled separately. |
| 01:15 – 01:30 | Break | Real break. People need it. |
| 01:30 – 02:15 | Exercises 3–4 — Archetype + long-form purpose sentence | Pair up, swap purpose statements, critique each other's verbs. |
| 02:15 – 02:30 | Exercise 5 — Purpose words | Fast and fun. Wordstorm. |
| 02:30 – 03:00 | Exercise 6 — Superpower | The hardest exercise emotionally. Allow space; don't rush. |
| 03:00 – 03:45 | Exercise 7 — Strategic Cascade | The hardest exercise intellectually. Plenary on one volunteer's cascade — model the coherence check. |
| 03:45 – 04:00 | Exercise 8 — Pillars | Quick. By now they should land. |
| 04:00 – 04:15 | Wrap | Each founder reads their 3–7-word purpose sentence aloud. Applause. Done. |

## Facilitator moves

- **Insist on specificity.** "Small businesses" is not a stakeholder; "a 35-person dental clinic owner who is also their bookkeeper" is.
- **Push back on jargon.** "Synergy", "ecosystem", "innovate" — none of these survive the workshop. If a founder uses one, ask: "What is the specific noun and verb under that word?"
- **Let people sit with the Superpower exercise.** It's the most personal one. If someone gets quiet, that's data — not a problem to solve fast.
- **Use the cascade coherence check publicly on at least one volunteer.** Founders learn the discipline by watching it done aloud once.
- **End with the short purpose sentence read aloud.** It's the single most galvanizing moment of the day.

## Common stuck points and unsticks

| Stuck point | Unstick |
|---|---|
| "I have too many stakeholders, I can't pick three." | Ask: "Which three would the business *fail* without?" — not which are most numerous. |
| "Functional, emotional, social — they all blur." | Reframe: do / feel / signal. Three verbs. Done. |
| "I don't know which archetype." | Read the five aloud. The first one they nodded at is usually right. The intellectual second-guess is usually wrong. |
| "My purpose sentence sounds corporate." | Strip every word that isn't a specific noun or a specific verb. What's left is the purpose. |
| "I can't write a Superpower without sounding arrogant." | Ask for evidence, not claims. "Tell me a moment in the last 90 days where you saw this." |
| "The cascade has all five cells but they don't connect." | Read top-to-bottom aloud. Ask: "Does cell 4 deliver cell 3? Does cell 5 keep cell 4 sharp?" Stop at the first break. |
| "I have 7 pillars." | Ask: "Which two are implied by another?" Cut. |

## Optional Exercises 9 and 10

For purpose-driven, mission-driven, or social-impact brands, run `/brand-values` (Brian Hickling's 4-step values process) and `/brand-movement` after the core 8. These add ~45 minutes each and are worth it for B-Corp-style and movement-led brands. For most other brands, these can be skipped or done later.

## After the workshop

- Each founder runs `/brand-export` and gets a printable HTML deck.
- A week later, send a check-in: "Has anything from the workshop made you change a decision yet?" That single email separates founders who wrote a workbook from founders who built a brand.
- Three months later: revisit. Brand DNA is a living document; some answers will have hardened, some will need a rewrite.
