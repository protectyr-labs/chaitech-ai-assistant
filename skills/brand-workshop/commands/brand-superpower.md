# /brand-superpower

Exercise 6. Three questions; the brand's superpower lives at their intersection.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Sections 1–5 should be filled, but allow override.

## The three questions

Ask them **one at a time**. Do not bundle.

### Q1 — What does the world need most?

> "Big-frame question first. In the world your brand operates in, what is most missing? What do customers, citizens, or peers most need that they are not getting?"

Push for specificity. "More security" is too broad; "affordable, trustworthy cybersecurity for businesses too small for a CISO" is right-sized.

Use the founder's stakeholders (section 1) and needs (section 2) as evidence anchors. Reflect: "Based on what you said in section 2, here is one possible answer — does that match your gut?"

### Q2 — What are you (your company) uniquely gifted at?

> "Now turn the camera on yourselves. Of the things the world needs, which one are you actually, demonstrably, *good* at? Not aspirationally — provably. What can you point to?"

Insist on evidence. Pull from the workspace if available — `README.md`, case-study notes, any prior client work. "I see you mentioned X in your README — does that count?"

If the founder cannot point to evidence yet (pre-launch), reframe:

> "Pre-launch is fine. Then the question is: what is your unfair advantage? A skill, a network, a piece of IP, a lived experience nobody else on the team has. Whatever makes 'we can do this' a defensible claim."

### Q3 — What are you passionate about?

> "Last question. Of the things you are good at — which one would you keep doing even if the money got hard? Where is the energy?"

If the founder gives a corporate answer ("we are passionate about customer success"), push: "What about it lights you up? Tell me the moment you knew you wanted to do this for the next ten years."

## Locate the superpower

After all three answers are written, name the intersection in plain language:

> "So your superpower is: <one sentence that combines all three>."

Example shape: *Compressing enterprise-grade security into something an SMB can afford and understand, fueled by 15 years of doing this for banks who never thanked us properly.*

## Write

```markdown
## 6. Superpower

### What the world needs most
<one paragraph>

### What we are uniquely gifted at
<one paragraph; cite evidence>

### What we are passionate about
<one paragraph; the founder's voice, not corporate>

### Our superpower (intersection)
> <one strong sentence>
```

## Constraints

- Refuse "we are passionate about excellence" and similar empty phrases. Ask once more for the specific moment.
- The intersection sentence is the deliverable. If two of three questions are filled but the intersection cannot be written, the exercise is not done. Loop back.
- Do not recycle the long-form purpose sentence (section 4) as the superpower. The two are related but the superpower is *what makes you the right team for that purpose.*
