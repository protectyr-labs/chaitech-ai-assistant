# /brand-not-doing

Captures section 13 of `STATE.md` — explicit "what we will not do." Pulled out of the cascade as its own exercise because a boundary list is a load-bearing artifact, not a footnote.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Sections 4 (purpose) and 8 (cascade) should be filled. The "will not do" list often crystallizes after those — but allow override since some founders come in already clear about what they refuse.

## Frame (two sentences)

> "Strategy is what you say no to. We're going to make a 3–5 item list of the things you've explicitly decided are out of scope. These items shape every customer conversation and every hire from now on."

## The conversation

1. Ask:

   > "List 3 to 5 things you've decided your brand will not do. Categories of customer you won't serve. Channels you won't sell on. Tones you won't take. Trade-offs you refuse to make. Anything specific you've decided is off the table."

2. If the founder gives soft answers ("we won't be sleazy"), push for concrete:

   > "Give me the specific version. 'Won't be sleazy' is everyone. What's the specific behavior you've ruled out that other people in your category routinely do?"

3. If the founder names exclusions you can already see in their cascade (section 8 "Will not play"), surface them:

   > "I noticed in section 8 you said you wouldn't sell to Fortune 500. That belongs here too. Should we add it explicitly?"

4. Test each line. For each "will not do" item, ask:

   > "Has anyone offered you the chance to do this in the last six months? If yes — turning it down was easy or hard? The hard turndowns are the most important items here."

## Write

```markdown
## 13. What We Will NOT Do

1. **<Short name>** — <1 sentence elaboration; specific>
2. **<Short name>** — ...
3. **<Short name>** — ...
4. (optional) ...
5. (optional) ...
```

## Wrap

> "Written to section 13. Re-read this list any time a tempting opportunity feels off. The hardest 'no's get easier when they are pre-decided. Next: `/brand-pillars` — the things your brand must keep doing to live its purpose."

## Constraints

- 3 minimum, 5 maximum. Below 3 is incomplete; above 5 is decoration.
- Each item must be specific enough that a third party could check whether you violated it.
- "Won't be evil" is not specific. "Won't take meetings with prospects under $50K ARR potential in 2026" is specific.
- If the founder's list overlaps with the cascade (section 8 "Will not play"), keep both — but flag the overlap so they know it's intentional reinforcement, not duplication.
