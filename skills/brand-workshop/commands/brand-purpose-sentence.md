# /brand-purpose-sentence

Exercise 4. Five fields. Done correctly, this is the single most-quoted artifact from the whole workshop.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Section 3 (Purpose Archetype) should be filled. Point the founder back if not.

## The five fields

Walk through them in order. **One field at a time.** Read each prompt, wait for the answer, refine, then move on.

### Field 1 — We exist to ...

> "Finish this sentence: *We exist to ___.* What is the verb? What is the change you make in the world?"

Push for a verb that matches the chosen archetype.
- *Reduce Friction* → "make X simpler", "eliminate Y", "remove the burden of Z"
- *Foster Prosperity* → "help X succeed", "grow", "support"
- *Enable Potential* → "unlock", "free", "make possible"
- *Encourage Exploration* → "open", "invite", "guide"
- *Kindle Happiness* → "delight", "lift", "spark"

### Field 2 — by ...

> "Now: *by ___.* What is the unique mechanism — the *how* only you can do?"

Push past generic answers ("by working hard"). Anchor in something specific to the founder's actual capability stack.

### Field 3 — For the good of ...

> "*For the good of ___.* Who, ultimately, benefits? Not just the buyer — the chain of people whose lives get better when this works."

Use the top 3 stakeholders from section 1 as a starting list, but push wider — the people downstream of those stakeholders count too.

### Field 4 — How this impacts the people we serve ...

> "*If we do this well, what changes for them?* Describe the 'after' state. The before state is the pain they live with today; the after is what they get."

### Field 5 — How we make money sustainably ...

> "*How does this stay viable as a business?* Be specific. What do customers pay for, how often, and at what kind of margin?"

This is the field most founders try to skip. Insist gently — a purpose statement that does not pencil out is a hobby, not a brand.

## Reflect, then write

Read all five fields back to the founder as a continuous paragraph:

> "So your purpose statement reads: *We exist to <field 1>, by <field 2>, for the good of <field 3>. When we do this well, <field 4>. We sustain this by <field 5>.* Does that sound like you?"

Iterate until they say yes.

## Write

```markdown
## 4. Purpose Sentence (long form)

- **We exist to:** ...
- **by:** ...
- **For the good of:** ...
- **How this impacts the people we serve:** ...
- **How we make money sustainably:** ...

### Read it as a paragraph
> We exist to ... by ... for the good of ... When we do this well, ... We sustain this by ...
```

## Wrap

> "Written to section 4. Next is `/brand-purpose-words` — building a word cloud and a tight 3–7-word version of the sentence above."

## Constraints

- Do not let the founder paste corporate jargon ("synergy", "ecosystem", "innovate"). Push for one specific noun and one specific verb.
- Do not write the sentence for the founder. You can offer 2–3 alternative phrasings *for them to react to*, but the words must be theirs.
- If field 5 is "TBD" or "we will figure out monetization later", flag it gently: "That is okay for v1, but write 'TBD — revisit after pricing exercise' so future you remembers to come back."
