# /brand-pillars

Exercise 8. The "what we have to keep doing forever" list — three or four pillars, no more.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Sections 1–7 should be filled.

## Frame

> "Pillars are the few things your brand *must keep doing* to live its purpose. Not values (those come next). Not tactics (those change). Pillars are the load-bearing walls — three or four, never more — that everything else hangs from. If you remove one, the brand collapses."

## Conversation

1. Re-read aloud (paraphrase) the founder's purpose sentence (section 4) and superpower (section 6). Then ask:

   > "Given that purpose and that superpower, what are three or four things you *must* keep doing — every quarter, every hire, every offer — to stay true to it?"

2. If the founder gets stuck, offer the structural categories from the cascade (section 7) as prompts:
   - A pillar drawn from *how to win* (cell 3) — usually the differentiator that makes the strategy possible.
   - A pillar about *capabilities* (cell 4) — what you must keep building.
   - A pillar about *the customer* — the experience promise.
   - A pillar about *culture / values* — how you do the work.

3. For each pillar, force a one-sentence definition AND a one-sentence "what this looks like in practice":

   > "Pillar 1 in two sentences. First: what is it? Second: how would I see this pillar showing up in your work next month?"

4. After the pillars are drafted, run a stress test:

   > "Read pillar 1 aloud. If you violated it tomorrow — by accident or under pressure — would the brand still be itself? If yes, it is not a pillar. Drop it or sharpen it."

> - `/brand-export` — render a printable HTML deck."

## Constraints

- Hard cap at 4 pillars. If the founder offers 5, ask which one is implied by another and drop it. Five pillars is decoration; four is architecture.
- Do not allow generic pillar names ("Quality", "Customer-centricity", "Innovation"). Push for a specific, brand-owned phrasing.
- Each pillar must have the "in practice" sentence. Without it, the pillar is decoration.
- The pillars should map back to the cascade (section 7). If they do not, either the pillars or the cascade is wrong — surface the conflict to the founder.
