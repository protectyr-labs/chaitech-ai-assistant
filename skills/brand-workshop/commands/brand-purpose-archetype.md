# /brand-purpose-archetype

Exercise 3. Five archetypes; pick the one that fits, plus a secondary if useful.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Sections 1 and 2 should be filled. If not, gently redirect, but allow override (some founders have purpose clarity before they have stakeholder clarity).

## Teach the five archetypes (once, briefly)

> "There are five common ways a company makes the world better. Most brands fit one strongly and a second one as a supporting note. Here they are, with one example each:
>
> 1. **Reduce Friction** — simplify, eliminate barriers. *Google. Spotify.*
> 2. **Foster Prosperity** — help others succeed. *Pampers. Warby Parker.*
> 3. **Enable Potential** — inspire greater possibilities. *Tesla. Nike.*
> 4. **Encourage Exploration** — champion discovery. *Airbnb. Adobe.*
> 5. **Kindle Happiness** — incite joy. *Dove. Zappos.*"

## Conversation

1. Ask:

   > "When you read those five out loud, which one made you nod first? Which one felt closest to *why* you are building this?"

2. Once they pick, test it:

   > "Tell me a moment in the last 90 days where you saw your brand do that for a real person. If you cannot, the archetype probably is not right yet."

3. Ask about a secondary:

   > "Is there a second archetype that supports the first? It is fine if there is not."

4. Capture the *why*. One paragraph, in the founder's voice. Not corporate copy.

## Write

```markdown
## 3. Purpose Archetype

### Primary fit
**<Archetype name>** — <one sentence on why this fits>

### Secondary fit
**<Archetype name or "None">** — <one sentence>

### Why this archetype, not another
<Founder's own words. 3–5 sentences. A real moment. A real customer.>
```

## Wrap

> "Written to section 3. Next is `/brand-purpose-sentence` — turning the archetype into a defensible long-form purpose statement."

## Constraints

- Do not let the founder pick all five. The exercise fails if there is no choice. Push them to one, with at most one secondary.
- "Reduce Friction" is the most over-picked archetype, especially in tech. If the founder picks it, ask once: "Is the deeper unlock that you also enable potential / foster prosperity? Friction is sometimes the *how*, not the *why*."
- Do not make the founder defend the choice against a competitor. The exercise is about their reality, not the market's perception.
