# /brand-needs

Exercise 2 of the Brand Workshop. Three needs categories × three stakeholders = nine boxes to fill.

## Pre-checks

- If `brand-workshop/STATE.md` does not exist, tell the user to run `/brand-start` first and stop.
- Read `### Top 3` from section 1. If it is empty, tell the user to run `/brand-stakeholders` first and stop.
- Read `## 2. Needs`. If filled, ask whether to revise.

## Walk-through

For each of the three stakeholders, in order, ask three questions:

### Functional — what they need to *do*

> "What does **<stakeholder>** practically need to *do* with your help? Think of the actual job — not how they feel, just what they need to get done. Example for a coffee shop customer: 'order quickly during the morning rush, get an unfussy receipt, find a clean seat near a power outlet.'"

Capture as a 4–6 item bulleted list.

### Emotional — what they need to *feel*

> "And how do they need to *feel* while doing it? Example: 'confident this is the best espresso I will get today, taken seriously even when I order a small drip, not rushed even at 8:45 a.m.'"

Capture as a 3–5 item bulleted list.

### Social — what they need to *signal*

> "And what do they need to be able to *signal* to others by being your customer? Example: 'I have taste, I support local, I am the kind of person who knows where the good coffee is.'"

Capture as a 2–4 item bulleted list.

## Tactics for the conversation

- Do this **one stakeholder at a time** — finish all three need types for stakeholder 1 before moving to stakeholder 2. Otherwise the founder loses the thread.
- If the founder gives a generic answer ("they want value"), push gently: "What would that look like specifically for them?"
- Use existing context. If the founder's `README.md` describes user pain points, surface them: "I noticed your README says X — should that go in functional needs for stakeholder 2?"
- Reflect what you heard before writing. One sentence: "So for the dental clinic owner, the functional needs are: 1, 2, 3. Yes?"

## Wrap

Confirm in one line:

> "Written to section 2. Next is `/brand-purpose-archetype` — which of five impact patterns fits how your brand makes the world better."

Stop.

## Constraints

- Do not bundle the three need types. Walk through Functional first, then Emotional, then Social. Founders confuse them when asked all at once.
- Do not let the founder skip Social. It is the easiest one to skip and the most useful for marketing later.
- If the founder genuinely cannot find a Social need for one stakeholder, write "(none surfaced — revisit after Exercise 6)" rather than inventing one.
