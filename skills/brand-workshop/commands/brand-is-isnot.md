# /brand-is-isnot

Captures section 11 of `STATE.md` — two parallel lists: what the brand IS and what it is NOT. The IS NOT list is the heavier-lifting half.

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Sections 4 (long-form purpose) and 6 (short purpose sentence) should be filled. Without them, "is / is not" floats.

## Frame (two sentences)

> "Two short lists. Five to seven items each. The IS NOT list is usually more clarifying than the IS list — boundaries draw the brand faster than aspirations do. Don't skip it."

## The conversation

### Half A — Brand IS

1. Ask:

   > "Five to seven adjectives, postures, or short phrases that describe what your brand IS. Not what it makes — how it behaves. Examples: 'plain-spoken', 'patient', 'practitioner-led', 'unfussy', 'opinionated about quality but not about taste'."

2. Use existing context. Pull bolded purpose words from section 5 as starting candidates. Offer them as a head start.

3. Push for specific, ownable phrasings. "Friendly" is not specific; "warm without being familiar" is.

### Half B — Brand IS NOT

1. Ask:

   > "Now five to seven things your brand is NOT. The IS NOT list is the most clarifying half of this exercise. Examples: 'IS NOT corporate', 'IS NOT precious about its own cleverness', 'IS NOT chasing TikTok trends', 'IS NOT trying to be enterprise.'"

2. If the founder gets stuck, offer the heuristic:

   > "Look at the brands you respect in your space. What are they doing that you would never do? Each of those is an IS NOT line."

3. Push past easy negations. "IS NOT cheap" is not as useful as "IS NOT willing to undercut on quality to win on price."

## Reflect

Read both lists back as a single statement:

> "So — your brand IS [is list, comma-separated]. Your brand IS NOT [is-not list, comma-separated]. Does that read like you?"

Iterate until they say yes.

## Write

```markdown
## 11. Brand IS / IS NOT

### Our brand IS

- ...
- ... (5–7 items)

### Our brand IS NOT
- ...
- ...
- ... (5–7 items)
```

## Wrap

> "Written to section 11. This dual list is the fastest tool to keep on hand when copy, design, or hiring decisions feel ambiguous — read it before you write or commit. Next: `/brand-tension` for the productive tension the brand navigates."

## Constraints

- 5–7 items per list. Less than 5 is incomplete; more than 7 is decoration.
- The IS NOT list is non-optional. If the founder tries to skip it, push once: "What are you not? It's the most useful half."
- Do not allow generic items ("Quality", "Innovation"). Push for specific, brand-owned phrasing.
- Avoid mirroring. The IS NOT list should not just be the IS list with "not" in front. They should describe different territories.
