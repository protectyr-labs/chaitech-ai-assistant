# /brand-purpose-words

Exercise 5. Two halves: divergent (cast wide), then convergent (compress to a phrase).

## Pre-checks

- `brand-workshop/STATE.md` must exist.
- Section 4 should be filled. The word cloud is much easier when the long-form sentence already exists.

## Half A — Word cloud (20–35 words)

1. Frame the exercise:

   > "Now we collect words. Adjectives, nouns, verbs — anything that describes what your brand *is* or how it *acts*. We are aiming for 20 to 35. Some will be obvious from your purpose sentence. Some will surprise you."

2. Seed the list. Read the founder's purpose sentence (section 4) back to them and pull 5–10 candidate words from it. Offer them as starters.

3. Push for variety. Suggest categories if they get stuck:
   - **What we are** (e.g., honest, scrappy, modern, classical, irreverent, careful)
   - **How we act** (e.g., fast, thorough, generous, decisive, measured)
   - **What we sound like** (e.g., warm, plain-spoken, witty, calm, direct)
   - **What we are not** (sometimes easier — *not* slick, *not* corporate, *not* preachy)

4. When the list is over 20, stop adding and ask the founder to highlight the 5–8 that feel most non-negotiable. Mark those as `**bold**` in the file.

## Half B — Short purpose sentence (3–7 words)

1. Frame:

   > "Take your bolded words and assemble a 3-to-7-word version of your purpose. Not a tagline yet — a north star. *Make security defensible.* *Eliminate ugly invoices.* *Coffee for the curious.* That kind of compression."

2. Offer 3–5 candidate phrasings. Make them deliberately different in tone (one verb-led, one noun-led, one playful, one austere). Let the founder react.

3. Land on **one primary** and **2–3 alternates** for later A/B in marketing.

## Write

```markdown
## 5. Purpose Words

### Word list
accessible, **bold**, plain-spoken, **defensible**, modern, ...
(20–35 words; the 5–8 most non-negotiable are bolded)

### Short purpose sentence (3–7 words)
- **Primary:** "..."
- Alternate: "..."
- Alternate: "..."
- Alternate: "..."
```

## Wrap

> "Written to section 5. Next is `/brand-superpower` — the intersection of what the world needs, what you are uniquely good at, and what you love."

## Constraints

- 35 is the cap. If the founder offers 50, ask which 35 are the strongest. Brand DNA work is about choice; an unbounded list is a non-answer.
- The primary 3–7-word sentence must contain a **verb** or a **specific noun**. Pure adjective stacks ("clear, honest, direct") are mood, not purpose.
- Do not turn the short sentence into a tagline. Taglines come later, in marketing. This is an internal compass.
